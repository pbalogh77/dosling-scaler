HQ3x fallback provenance
=======================

src/hq_block.c derives from native/vendor/hqx/hq3x.c, whose upstream and pinned
revision are recorded in native/vendor/hqx/ORIGIN.txt. Copyright Maxim Stepin
(2003) and Cameron Zemek (2010). LGPL-2.1-or-later; full license in
native/vendor/hqx/COPYING. Original notices remain in the derived source.

Modifications for this student (2026-10-17): isolated one 3x3 source block's
HQ3x switch into ds_hq3x_block; added ds_hq3x_block_cached to accept precomputed
YUV and the original 8-bit HQ key. The 176 additional neighbor-pair comparisons
use cached YUV with identical thresholds. The pattern switch and original
interpolation macros are preserved. A conformance test checks the isolated
fallback against the complete HQ3x renderer, including borders. common.h's
exact integer YUV conversion is the project's earlier recorded modification.

This derivative is compiled into the separate replaceable dos_lut_hqx shared
library/framework. Its source, dependent headers, upstream AUTHORS and license
are included in CMake install output. The student links it dynamically; no
full-frame teacher library is needed by the student runtime. Keep the library
replaceable and comply with the LGPL when packaging/distributing an app.
